About

RemRoo is a Seattle-born Jewish artist, going to WMS in Seattle's Central District. He has created one album, consisting of two songs so far. He gets his inspiration from songs on his daily walk to the bus, or from failed song attempts. His top songs include Summer Day (Ends at Noon) and Love (All That You Need).
His top album is Kahoot!ing, which also contains his top 2 songs. He also enjoys programming, vexillology (the study of flags), bsaeball, football, basketball, and cricket.
